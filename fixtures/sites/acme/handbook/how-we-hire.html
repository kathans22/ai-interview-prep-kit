How we hire

Our process is four stages and takes about two weeks end to end. We tell you where you stand after every stage, and we never ask for unpaid work beyond the take-home.

1. Intro call (30 minutes)

A conversation with the hiring manager about what you have built and what you want next. No whiteboard, no trivia.

2. Take-home (about three hours)

A small, self-contained problem close to something we actually do — usually reconciling optimistic updates against a server that sometimes rejects them. We pay for your time on this stage.

3. Technical conversation (60 minutes)

We walk through your take-home together and extend it. We are interested in how you reason about failure, back-pressure and data that arrives out of order.

4. System design and values (60 minutes)

A design discussion grounded in our domain — for example, an operator console receiving 400 concurrent vehicle updates — followed by a conversation about how you work with other people.

What we assess

  • Depth in React and TypeScript, especially state that changes under you
  • Judgement about degradation: what happens when a dependency is slow or gone
  • Written communication — we are remote-first and write more than we talk
  • Mentoring: most senior hires here support two or three engineers

Interview process feedback

We ask every candidate what the process was like, and we publish changes we make as a result in this handbook.
