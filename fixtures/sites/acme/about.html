About Acme

Founded in 2019 by two former dispatchers, Acme grew out of a spreadsheet that re-planned delivery routes when a depot lost power. We are 38 people across six countries, funded by revenue since 2023.

What we value

  • Write it down — decisions live in the handbook, not in anyone's head
  • Degrade honestly — a late lorry is a fact, not something to hide from a customer
  • Own the whole thing — the person who builds it runs it
